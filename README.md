## 🐾 The Story

**World Rally Cats** is an immersive tale set in the Cat World Rally Championship (CWRC), following the psychological journey of **Kuro "The Shadow" Nakamura**, a methodical Bombay cat and three-time world champion. 

The story explores the tension between **mathematical precision** and **passionate commitment** in motorsport, as Kuro grapples with an existential question that haunts every dominant champion: *Do you deserve victory if you never risk losing it?*

### 🏆 Championship Psychology

Through six meticulously crafted chapters, we witness Kuro's internal struggle as he:
- Wins championships through calculated consistency rather than raw speed
- Questions whether protection-based driving diminishes the meaning of victory  
- Faces rivals who drive with passion while he drives with arithmetic
- Searches for the "third prayer" he left unspoken at Ise Grand Shrine

The narrative combines **technical rally accuracy** with **literary depth**, creating a unique exploration of what it means to be the best when being the best feels hollow.

## 🌍 World Building

The **CWRC Lorebook** contains:

### 🏁 The Racing Universe
- **Complete 2026 season** with 13 rally locations across 4 continents  
- **Three manufacturer teams**: Toyota Gazoo Racing, Hyundai Shell Mobis WRT, M-Sport Ford WRT
- **Detailed technical specifications** for Rally1 hybrid machinery
- **Cat-specific adaptations** for tailed anatomy and enhanced senses

### 🐱 Character Profiles
- **Six main drivers** with distinct personalities, driving styles, and backgrounds
- **Co-driver partnerships** and team dynamics
- **Detailed championship standings** and rally-by-rally results
- **Cat-themed incidents** that blend humor with competitive tension

### 🏆 Racing Philosophy  
- **Kaizen-based precision** (Toyota's continuous improvement philosophy)
- **Passionate attack driving** (Hyundai's emotional intensity)  
- **Privateer resourcefulness** (M-Sport's budget optimization)
- **The mathematics of survival** vs. **the mythology of speed**
