# Cat World Rally Championship 2026 - Lorebook

## Introduction to the CWRC Universe

**The Cat World Rally Championship (CWRC)** represents the pinnacle of feline motorsport, where anthropomorphic cats pilot Rally1 hybrid machines across the world's most demanding terrain. The 2026 season marks the sport's third year under hybrid regulations, with three manufacturer teams fielding six full-time entries plus occasional wildcards.

**Technical specifications:** All Rally1 cars feature 1.6-liter turbocharged engines producing approximately 380 horsepower, supplemented by a 100kW (134 hp) electric motor for short bursts. Total system output reaches 500 horsepower. Cars weigh 1,260 kg minimum and utilize sophisticated all-wheel-drive systems with paddle-shifted sequential gearboxes.

**The 2026 Calendar comprises 13 rounds** spanning gravel, tarmac, snow, and mixed-surface events across four continents.

**Species:** Bombay cat (Felis catus - Bombay breed)
**Age:** 29 years
**Nationality:** Japanese

He calculates optimal racing lines using geometric precision, conserving tires and hybrid energy for crucial moments. His throttle control is so smooth that onboard cameras appear stabilized. **Weaknesses:** Occasionally too cautious on gravel when aggressive commitment pays off

**Pre-race ritual:** Arrives at service park exactly 90 minutes before first stage. Walks the perimeter of the Toyota garage three times clockwise. Reviews pacenotes in complete silence. Adjusts seat position to millimeter precision using a custom measuring tool.

**Notable quote:** "The fastest way to finish is to never make a mistake."

**Co-driver:** Akira "Echo" Sato (Bombay cat, 27, male) - Kuro's childhood friend from Nagoya. Delivers pacenotes with metronomic consistency and perfect pitch. ## Rally-by-Rally Results & Notable Events

### Round 1: Rallye Monte-Carlo (January 23-26)

**Location:** Gap/Monaco, France
**Surface:** Mixed tarmac/snow/ice
**Stages:** 18 stages, 389.73 km competitive distance

**Pre-Event Drama:** Monte-Carlo's unpredictable weather forecasts split teams on tire strategies. Toyota opts for conservative approach. Hyundai gambles on aggressive studded tire allocation. M-Sport splits strategies between crews.

**Rally Highlights:**

**Thursday Night (Gap):** Opening super-special stage through Gap's city streets. Thousands of feline spectators pack the barriers, their night-vision eyes glowing in darkness. Kuro sets fastest time by 0.3 seconds despite Thierry's aggressive attack.

*Cat-themed incident:* During SS2 (Col du Perty), Grégoire Munster encounters a colony of feral mountain cats blocking the road. Rather than wait, he performs a spectacular handbrake turn around them. "The cousins needed right-of-way," he jokes. Loses 15 seconds but gains viral social media fame.

**Friday:** Mixed conditions wreak havoc. Col de Turini features ice in shadows, dry tarmac in sun. Ott Tänak showcases his Nordic mastery, taking 4 of 6 stage wins. His tire preservation is surgical—runs same set all day while others change twice.

*Cat-themed incident:* Sami Pajari's youthful aggression costs him on SS6. Attacks a hairpin too aggressively, slides wide, and his tail gets caught momentarily in the door mechanism. "Note to self," Enni deadpans, "tuck tail before Turini." They lose 8 seconds but continue.

**Saturday:** Battle intensifies between Ott and Thierry. The Estonian's ice-king precision versus Belgian's tarmac mastery. Gap fluctuates between 1.2 and 3.8 seconds across eight stages. Kuro lurks in third, waiting for mistakes.

*Cat-themed incident:* During SS12 (Vaumeilh–Claret), Adrien Fourmaux's heterochromia proves advantageous. His blue eye adapts faster to shadow sections, amber eye handles bright snow glare. He sets third-fastest time. "Finally my weird eyes pay off," he purrs.

**Sunday:** Final four stages. Ott leads by 2.1 seconds entering Power Stage. Thierry attacks desperately—too desperately. Overshoots junction 200m from finish, loses 4.3 seconds. Ott wins Monte Carlo for the second time in his career.

**Final Classification:**

1. **Ott Tänak/Martin Järveoja (Hyundai)** - 3:28:44.2
2. **Thierry Neuville/Martijn Wydaeghe (Hyundai)** - +8.7s
3. **Kuro Nakamura/Akira Sato (Toyota)** - +24.3s
4. **Adrien Fourmaux/Alexandre Coria (M-Sport Ford)** - +1:12.8
5. **Sami Pajari/Enni Mälkönen (Toyota)** - +1:45.2
6. **Grégoire Munster/Louis Louka (M-Sport Ford)** - +3:22.1

**Power Stage Winner:** Ott Tänak (+5 bonus points)

**Post-Rally:** Ott's victory speech consists of eleven words: "Car was good. Martin was good. Hyundai was good. Thank you." Crowd erupts anyway.

**Championship Standings After Round 1:**
1. Ott Tänak - 30 pts

### Round 2: Rally Sweden (February 13-16)

**Location:** Umeå, Sweden
**Surface:** Snow and ice
**Stages:** 19 stages, 316.94 km competitive distance

**Pre-Event Drama:** Teams debate optimal studded tire length. Frozen lake stages demand different approach than forest sections. Ott arrives as defending Sweden winner and favorite. Sami Pajari competes on home Nordic territory, desperate to prove himself.

**Rally Highlights:**

**Thursday:** Umeå super-special stage packed with Scandinavian fans. Kuro wears special reflective racing suit that shimmers under arena lights—"like racing moonlight," one announcer gushes. He edges Ott by 0.1s.

*Cat-themed incident:* During SS2 (Umeå Sprint), a spectator's pet Norwegian Forest Cat escapes and wanders onto stage. Sami Pajari spots it 200m ahead, instinctively yowls a warning. The cat scampers safely away. Sami still sets third-fastest time. "Cousin was just sightseeing," he grins.

**Friday:** Pure winter warfare. Temperatures hit -15°C. Sami Pajari unleashes spectacular performance on home snow—takes three stage wins through commitment that borders on insanity. His Scandinavian flick technique draws roars from Finnish fans who drove across border.

Ott and Kuro engage in psychological chess match. Neither attacks; both preserve tires and hybrid energy. The Estonian leads by 3.2s after eight stages through sheer efficiency.

*Cat-themed incident:* Thierry Neuville's Korat heritage proves disadvantageous. Korats evolved in warm Thailand; his paws go partially numb in extreme cold despite heated driving shoes. He finishes Friday in fifth, frustrated and cold. Team wraps his feet in heated blankets during service. "I am ice cream," he mutters.

**Saturday:** Frozen lake stages showcase different techniques. Ott's minimal steering inputs preserve momentum. Kuro's geometric precision finds optimal lines. Sami's raw aggression entertains but costs him with a 360-degree spin on SS13 (Långed). He recovers instantly—"just checking the view!"—but loses 18 seconds.

Meanwhile, Adrien Fourmaux delivers career-best snow performance. His Van breed's swimming ability translates to unexpected snow pace. "Water, snow, same principle—stay afloat," he explains earnestly. Climbs to fourth overall.

*Cat-themed incident:* Grégoire Munster challenges local reindeer herd to impromptu drag race during transport section. The reindeer win. Malcolm Wilson's exasperated radio message: "Grégoire, they have four-wheel-drive too, you know."

**Sunday:** Final four stages including Torsby Power Stage. Ott leads Kuro by 8.4s—manageable if Kuro attacks. But the Shadow calculates: securing second place guarantees points consistency, attacking risks mistake. He maintains gap, accepts second.

Sami Pajari attacks Power Stage with abandon, sets fastest time by 2.1 seconds. Finnish fans erupt. "THIS is my home!" he roars, standing on car roof during podium ceremony.

**Final Classification:**

1. **Ott Tänak/Martin Järveoja (Hyundai)** - 2:48:22.1
2. **Kuro Nakamura/Akira Sato (Toyota)** - +8.4s
3. **Sami Pajari/Enni Mälkönen (Toyota)** - +42.7s
4. **Adrien Fourmaux/Alexandre Coria (M-Sport Ford)** - +1:18.9
5. **Thierry Neuville/Martijn Wydaeghe (Hyundai)** - +1:45.3
6. **Grégoire Munster/Louis Louka (M-Sport Ford)** - +2:51.4

**Power Stage Winner:** Sami Pajari (+5 bonus points)

**Post-Rally:** Sami's victory howl echoes across frozen lake. He performs donuts on ice until Toyota engineers physically restrain him. Ott's celebration involves slight smile—team photographs it as historic moment.

**Championship Standings After Round 2:**
1. Ott Tänak - 55 pts

### Round 3: Safari Rally Kenya (March 27-30)

**Location:** Nairobi, Kenya
**Surface:** Rough gravel, volcanic rock, fesh-fesh dust
**Stages:** 18 stages, 367.22 km competitive distance

**Pre-Event Drama:** Safari's brutality worries all teams. Rock damage, dust, wildlife encounters create unique challenges. Teams install heavy-duty underbody protection. Thierry arrives determined to reclaim championship momentum. Kuro arrives with enhanced cooling systems—black fur absorbs heat dangerously.

**Rally Highlights:**

**Thursday:** Nairobi super-special stage through city streets. Local cat population swells service park—thousands of African wildcats, caracals, and servals watch in awe. Grégoire sets fastest time through pure commitment. "This place smells like ADVENTURE!" he yowls.

*Cat-themed incident:* During reconnaissance, Kuro's convoy encounters pride of lions near SS3 route. The Bombay cat and African lions share long, respectful stare. "They gave nod of approval," Akira reports seriously. "Predators recognize predators."

**Friday:** Absolute chaos. Fesh-fesh dust sections reduce visibility to near-zero. Ott leads but Toyota's Kuro struggles—his black fur absorbs heat, internal temperature rises dangerously. Team installs emergency ventilation system during midday service. Sami Pajari drives with windows fully open despite dust. "I want to FEEL Africa!" he shouts.

Thierry attacks with Belgian fury, taking four stage wins. His silver-blue Korat coloring reflects sunlight better, keeping him cooler. Gap to Ott shrinks to 12.3s.

*Cat-themed incident:* During SS7 (Oserian), Adrien Fourmaux's heterochromia causes problems—dust coats his blue eye's contact lens differently than amber eye. Vision becomes asymmetrical. He stops mid-stage, removes lenses, continues squinting. Loses 45 seconds but finishes. "Note to self: goggles next year," Alexandre mutters.

**Saturday:** Safari shows teeth. SS10 (Kedong) features volcanic rock that shreds tires. Grégoire Munster punctures twice in one stage, continues on rim for final 3km. Sparks fly dramatically. He finishes stage, parks car, and it immediately catches fire from rim heat. He escapes, stands watching his Puma burn, and shrugs: "Well, that was new experience."

M-Sport withdraws Munster (car totaled), rallies Fourmaux carefully.

Ott and Thierry battle intensifies. The Belgian attacks Sleeping Warrior stage (SS12) with pure aggression, takes 8.2s from Ott. Gap now just 4.1s entering Sunday.

*Cat-themed incident:* Kuro encounters zebras during SS11. His predatory instincts momentarily activate—pupils dilate, tail twitches. Akira's sharp "FOCUS!" snaps him back. They lose 0.3s but avoid embarrassing headlines about world champion chasing zebras.

**Sunday:** Final five stages including Hell's Gate Power Stage. Thierry attacks relentlessly. Ott defends with Nordic ice—every corner perfect, every landing precise. Gap fluctuates between 2.8s and 5.1s across four stages.

Power Stage showdown: Thierry needs stage win plus Ott finishing outside top-5 bonus positions. The Belgian ATTACKS. Jumps are massive, dust clouds biblical. He sets fastest time—but Ott finishes second on Power Stage, securing 4 bonus points. Ott wins Safari by 3.7 seconds.

**Final Classification:**

1. **Ott Tänak/Martin Järveoja (Hyundai)** - 3:52:18.3
2. **Thierry Neuville/Martijn Wydaeghe (Hyundai)** - +3.7s
3. **Kuro Nakamura/Akira Sato (Toyota)** - +2:45.8
4. **Sami Pajari/Enni Mälkönen (Toyota)** - +4:12.6
5. **Adrien Fourmaux/Alexandre Coria (M-Sport Ford)** - +6:38.2
DNF: **Grégoire Munster** (fire damage, SS12)

**Power Stage Winner:** Thierry Neuville (+5 bonus points)

**Post-Rally:** Ott sprays water (not champagne—limited supply in Kenya) from podium. His smile reaches "medium setting." Thierry fumes—"3.7 seconds! TROIS POINT SEPT!" Team restrains him from kicking tire barrier.

**Championship Standings After Round 3:**
1. Ott Tänak - 84 pts
2. Thierry Neuville - 51 pts
3. Kuro Nakamura - 48 pts
4. Sami Pajari - 37 pts

### Round 4: Rally Croatia (April 17-20)

**Location:** Zagreb, Croatia
**Surface:** Smooth tarmac
**Stages:** 20 stages, 316.84 km competitive distance

**Pre-Event Drama:** First pure tarmac rally of season. Thierry arrives as tarmac specialist, determined to cut Ott's points lead. Kuro brings new hybrid deployment strategy specifically for tarmac. Adrien views Croatia as breakthrough opportunity—tarmac is his specialty.

**Rally Highlights:**

**Thursday:** Zagreb Arena super-special stage. Home-crowd favorite is Croatian wildcard entry Filip Mareš (local European Rally Cat), but stars dominate. Thierry sets fastest time, roars victory howl that echoes through arena. "This is MY surface!"

## Glossary of Rally Terms (for continued lorebook use)

**Scandinavian flick:** Pendulum technique where car is steered away from corner before flicking toward apex, using weight transfer to rotate car

**Pacenotes:** Co-driver's instructions describing upcoming corners (e.g., "Right-4 tightens to 3, crest, 100 to left-5")

**Recce:** Reconnaissance, where crews drive stages at road speed before rally to prepare notes

**Shakedown:** Non-competitive stage before rally where crews test setup

**Service park:** Designated area where mechanics work on cars between stages

**Power Stage:** Final stage offering bonus championship points (5-4-3-2-1 for top-5)

**Hybrid deployment:** Strategic use of electric motor boost, limited duration per stage

**Road position:** Starting order, critical on gravel (later = cleaner, faster roads)

**SS:** "Special Stage" - competitive timed section
